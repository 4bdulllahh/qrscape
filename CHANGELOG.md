# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-11)

First release.

- 15 seeded voxel scenes that are also scannable QR codes, 5 moods, Grow another one.
- Hover or Show QR code turns the world into a flat QR code.
- GIF and PNG downloads for email and chat.
- Copy-paste website code (`<qr-scape>`) that loops 5 s of scenery and 2.5 s of QR code, and shows the QR on hover.
- Light and dark themes, phone layout, logo, icons and share image.
