## QRscape 0.2.0: 30 worlds, with life in them

Twice as many worlds, from a weeping willow to a Venice canal, and little creatures now live in them. The page also fits on one laptop screen.

### New worlds

- **15 new scenes.** Pine, baobab, palm island, weeping willow, tree fern, waterfall, hot spring, rice terraces, Mount Fuji, lily pond, Kyoto pagoda, lantern street, Chongqing hillside, Venice canal and Santorini.
- **Nature and City groups.** The scene list is split into two groups, and you can type to search it.
- **Longer links, more detail.** A longer link makes a bigger QR code, and the world fills it with more trees, houses, boats and stones.

### Life in the world

- **Little creatures.** Butterflies, birds, dragonflies, falling petals, swimming fish and rising steam, picked to suit each scene.
- **Out of the way when it counts.** Creatures fade out before the world turns into a QR code, so it still scans. They also show in the GIF and on your website.
- **Calm when asked.** Creatures hold still if your device asks for less motion.

### Better scenes

- **Every old scene redone.** A bushier tree, four kinds of bonsai (pine, maple, juniper, azalea), a chunkier cherry blossom, a natural koi pond, a panda in the bamboo, tables outside the tea house, and more.

### Easier to use

- **One screen on laptops.** Three tabs (Make it, Download, Website code) keep everything in view without scrolling.
- **Any size for your website.** Pick small, medium or large, or type any size from 80 to 1200 pixels.
- **Light by default.** The site opens in the light look, with a Dark button at the top.

### Fixed

- **Scene list arrow.** The arrow on the scene list sits in the right place.
- **Big lighthouse.** The lighthouse island draws properly on long links.
