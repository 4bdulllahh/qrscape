## QRscape 0.1.0: every link grows a little world

The first release. Paste a link and it grows into a calm 8-bit world that is also a QR code you can scan.

### Worlds

- **15 scenes.** Tree, bonsai, mountains, fish bowl, zen garden, cherry blossom, koi pond, bamboo grove, torii gate, tea house, coffee shop, tofu shop, ramen stall, lighthouse island and cactus pot.
- **Your link is the seed.** The same link always grows the same world, and a different link grows a different one.
- **Grow another one.** Get a fresh world from the same link with one click.
- **5 moods.** Day, Blossom, Autumn, Dusk and Night, chosen by your link or by you.
- **Gentle motion.** Leaves sway, water bobs and the world turns slowly. It all stays still if your device asks for less motion.

### The QR trick

- **Hover to reveal.** Hover over the world, or press Show QR code, and it turns into a flat QR code.
- **Checked to scan.** Every scene in every mood is checked with a real QR reader.

### Sharing

- **GIF for email.** A looping GIF of the world turning into its QR code and back, usually under 1 MB.
- **PNG.** A flat, crisp QR code in your world's colours.
- **Website code.** Copy one short snippet in small, medium or large. On your page it shows the world, then the QR code, and hovering shows the QR code.

### Look

- **Light and dark themes,** a two-column layout on big screens and one column on phones.
